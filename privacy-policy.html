Privacy Policy for TcpButtons

Last updated: August 29, 2026

TcpButtons ("the App") is a simple utility that lets you send TCP messages to a host and port that you configure. This Privacy Policy explains what happens to your information when you use the App.

No Data Collection

TcpButtons does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or to any third party. The App does not use analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking technologies of any kind.

Network Connections

The App sends TCP messages directly from your device to the host and port that you specify in the App's settings. These connections are made solely at your instruction, go directly to the destination you configure, and are never routed through, logged by, or accessible to the developer of this App.

If you enable the built-in local echo server, it runs entirely on your own device and does not send any data outside of your device.

Account Information

TcpButtons does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use the App.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-processing services.

Children's Privacy

TcpButtons does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated in the future, the revised version will be posted at this same URL with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the support page at https://github.com/0lamboat18/TcpButtons.
